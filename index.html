Moving Fortress TD

How to Play

  • WASD moves the 5x5 platform.
  • Buy towers and click empty platform cells to place them.
  • Drag towers to move them.
  • Drag two same-type same-level towers together to merge them.
  • Buy minions to help fight enemies.
  • Survive infinite waves.
  • Enemies get stronger every wave.
